# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Executives and their teams use Margen to find documents, review decisions and return contextual feedback to Claude, Codex or Hermes across devices. The library is a workspace; shared artifacts remain editorial reading surfaces.

## Product purpose
Connect artifacts, versions, comments and private notes under explicit permissions. Search content, connect topics and recover the real originating agent, session and device when recorded.

## Brand personality
Direct, precise and approachable. Reference the clarity of Linear, contextual review in Figma and capable data controls in Retool and shadcn/ui. Margen means the space around the document where observations become decisions.

## Anti-references
Large forms obstructing reading; verbose controls; nested decorative grids; crowded panels; infrastructure metadata exposed to readers. No fabricated relationships or implied semantic analysis without evidence.

## Design principles
- Find, recognize and resume before administering.
- Consistent previews across gallery, list, table and detail.
- Explainable classification and permission-aware relationships.
- Provenance per version, without invented historical sessions.
- Reader surfaces show the document; management belongs to the creator.

## Accessibility
Preserve keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, textual chart alternatives and 320/390 px reading. Never communicate status through color alone. Repository documentation is English; artifact content follows its audience.
