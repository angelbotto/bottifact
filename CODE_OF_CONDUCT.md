# Community conduct

Margen welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent, and make technical disagreements specific and constructive. Give people room to learn and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or unsolicited promotion. These expectations apply in project issues, discussions, reviews and other spaces maintained by this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to angel@botto.is. Include relevant links and context without exposing more personal information than necessary. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation or ban accounts to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate and applied consistently, including to maintainers.

This is the project's own concise code of conduct, not a claim of certification or external governance.
